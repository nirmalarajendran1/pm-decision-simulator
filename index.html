PM Decision Simulator

Product management case studies, but you make the call — then see a quantitative simulation of how it plays out, compared to what actually happened.

Simulated outcome

Illustrative model calibrated to Netflix's publicly reported quarterly results. Option A's numbers are close to the real reported figures by design; options B and C are this project's own reasoned estimates of how the same underlying mechanics would have played out under a different decision — not verified Netflix data.

Built as a prototype exploring what an interactive, outcome-simulating case study could look like for PM courses. New cases can be added as a single file under js/cases/ — no engine changes required.
